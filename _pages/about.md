I am a professor at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/) in the [Department of Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.mcgill.ca/mathstat/), and the inaugural director of the [McGill Computational and Data Systems Initiative (CDSI)](https://www.mcgill.ca/cdsi/). My research is focused on how statistical and machine learning theory and methods can support human endeavours enabled by computing and engineered systems, frequently from a network-based perspective of systems science. I collaborate regularly on problems in computational biology, computational neuroscience and, most recently, AI-assisted chemistry and materials science. I have published over one hundred articles, frequently with students and other coauthors, including several books on the topic of network analysis.

As an associate editor, I have had the pleasure of serving on the boards of JASA and JRSS-B in statistics, IEEE IP and TNSE in engineering, and SIMODS in mathematics. I formerly served as co-chair of the U.S. National Academies of Sciences, Medicine, and Engineering Roundtable on Data Science Education. Finally, I've been honored to be named an elected fellow of the AAAS, ASA and IMS, an elected senior member of IEEE, and an elected member of the ISI.
