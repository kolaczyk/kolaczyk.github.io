I am a professor at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/) in the [Department of Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.mcgill.ca/mathstat/), and the inaugural director of the [McGill Computational and Data Systems Initiative (CDSI)](https://www.mcgill.ca/cdsi/). I am also an associate academic member at [Mila, The Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/en).

My research is focused on how statistical and machine learning theory and methods can support human endeavours enabled by computing and engineered systems, frequently from a network-based perspective of systems science. I collaborate regularly on problems in computational biology, computational neuroscience and, most recently, AI-assisted chemistry and materials science. I have published over one hundred articles, frequently with students and other coauthors, and several books on the topic of network analysis.

As an associate editor, I have had the pleasure of serving on the boards of the [Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA)](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/uasa20) and the [Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B (JRSSB)](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb) in statistics, the [IEEE Transactions on Image Processing](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/ieee-transactions-image-processing) and the [IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering](https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-tnse) in engineering, and the [SIAM Journal on Mathematics of Data Science (SIMODS)](https://www.siam.org/publications/siam-journals/siam-journal-on-mathematics-of-data-science/) in mathematics. I formerly served as co-chair of the [U.S. National Academies of Sciences, Medicine, and Engineering Roundtable on Data Science Education](https://www.nationalacademies.org/our-work/roundtable-on-data-science-postsecondary-education). Finally, I've been honored to be named an elected fellow of the AAAS, ASA and IMS, an elected senior member of IEEE, and an elected member of the ISI.
